Surrogates and stationarity
===========================

.. currentmodule:: gyraph.surrogates

Tools for null-hypothesis testing on graph signals: generate signals that share
the graph power spectrum of your data but nothing else, and check the
stationarity assumption that makes those surrogates valid.
